Make your own Pokémon game

Ember Studio is a free editor for building your own game on top of Pokémon FireRed or Emerald: maps, story, animated scenes, Pokémon, trainers, items, text, graphics and music, with the game running right inside it.

Features

Everything in one place

No hex editors, no scripting languages, no ten different tools. You see the game, you click on it and you change it.

🗺

Maps and the world

Paint maps and see all the connected maps together. Drag them around, resize them, make new ones, and follow doors into houses.

👤

People, doors and signs

Place characters and edit what they say right on the map. Every conversation can open as a chat, with YES / NO branches side by side.

📜

Story

The original story as a timeline, and your own story written with blocks instead of code. "The journey" shows the whole game on one line.

🐾

Pokémon and legendaries

Stats, types, moves, evolutions and wild Pokémon. Put legendaries anywhere in the world, or turn a battle picture into a sprite.

⚔

Trainers and moves

Teams, levels, items and battle texts for every trainer, plus moves, TMs, HMs and tutors.

🎒

Items and shops

What each item does in plain words, with fields to change it. Draw item icons pixel by pixel, add new items and stock every shop.

💬

Text and languages

Every line of the game in one place, with a full Hebrew translation, right-to-left text and Hebrew letters in the game fonts.

🎨

Graphics and music

Sprites, characters made from a picture, the title screen, and songs imported from MIDI.

🎮

Play inside the editor

Start the game from any spot on the map with the built-in mGBA emulator, without leaving the editor.

🧭

Can the game be finished?

The game check walks the whole world like a player would. It tells you whether the league can be reached and where a player would get stuck.

🔍

Search everything

Press Ctrl+K to find any map, Pokémon, trainer, item, move or line of dialogue.

📁

Projects that fit in Git

A project keeps only your changes, never the ROM, so you can share it and work on it with others.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11.
  • Your own copy of Pokémon FireRed (US) v1.1 (CRC 84EE4776) or Pokémon Emerald (US). No ROM is included, and none will be.
  • The first time, Windows may warn because the file is not signed: choose More info → Run anyway.

Just want to look first? Try the editor in your browser: open your own ROM (it stays on your computer), edit it and play it there. Projects, Play from here and building need the download.
